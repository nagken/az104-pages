## How to use these videos

1. **First pass - John Savill Study Cram** (one sitting, 3-4 hours). It will surface every topic the exam can ask about and tell you which ones need deeper work.
2. **Second pass - Microsoft official playlist**. Use it for the topics Savill flagged as weak for you. Microsoft's videos go deeper and align 1:1 with the *Skills measured* outline.
3. **Third pass - topic deep dives on Savill's channel** (search by feature, e.g. "Azure Backup", "VNet peering"). Pair these with the relevant section of this study guide and the matching Microsoft Learn module.
4. Then take the **Practice Assessment** (see that page in the sidebar) before scheduling the real exam.

> Tip: turn on captions and play at 1.25-1.5x - Savill's diagrams are the value, the audio can be sped up safely.
